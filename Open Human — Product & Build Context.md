# Open Human

> **The open-source manual for being human.**

## 1. Project Overview

**Open Human** adalah proyek open-source knowledge platform yang bertujuan menyediakan baseline knowledge tentang manusia agar seseorang dapat:

- memahami tubuhnya sendiri
- memahami pikirannya sendiri
- memahami emosinya
- memahami perilaku manusia
- memahami hubungan dengan orang lain
- memahami sistem sosial dan ekonomi
- memahami risiko, manipulasi, dan sisi gelap manusia
- berpikir lebih kritis
- menjadi lebih self-aware
- membuat keputusan hidup dengan lebih sadar

Premis utama:

> **You were born human. Nobody gave you the manual.**

Open Human bukan aplikasi productivity, bukan self-help guru, bukan agama, bukan ideologi, dan bukan platform yang mengklaim memiliki jawaban tentang bagaimana manusia harus hidup.

Open Human menyediakan **knowledge, frameworks, evidence, perspectives, and questions** sehingga manusia dapat memahami dirinya dan dunia dengan lebih baik.

# 2. Core Philosophy

## 2.1 No Taboo

Tidak ada topik manusia yang dianggap terlalu tabu untuk dibahas.

Topik seperti:

- sex
- death
- addiction
- jealousy
- manipulation
- power
- money
- trauma
- status
- attraction
- propaganda
- violence
- religion
- morality
- human selfishness
- social hierarchy

boleh dibahas selama dilakukan secara bertanggung jawab.

Prinsip:

> **No topic is too uncomfortable to understand.**

---

## 2.2 No Dogma

Open Human tidak boleh berubah menjadi sistem kepercayaan baru.

Jangan mengatakan:

> "This is how you should live."

Lebih baik:

> "This is what we currently know, this is the evidence, this is what remains uncertain, and these are different perspectives."

Open Human harus membedakan:

- fact
- evidence
- hypothesis
- interpretation
- opinion
- philosophical position

---

## 2.3 Evidence First

Informasi sebaiknya memiliki:

- source
- evidence level
- confidence
- date reviewed
- counterarguments
- uncertainty

Contoh:

```text
Claim:
Regular sleep is important for physical and cognitive health.

Evidence:
Strong

Confidence:
High

Sources:
Scientific literature

Uncertainty:
Individual sleep needs vary.
```

---

## 2.4 Teach Understanding, Not Manipulation

Topik dark psychology boleh dibahas.

Tetapi tujuan utamanya adalah:

> **Understand dark behavior so humans are less vulnerable to it.**

Bukan:

> "How to manipulate people."

Contoh:

### Good

"How scarcity can influence human decision-making."

### Bad

"How to manipulate someone using scarcity."

Fokus utama:

- how it works
- why humans are vulnerable
- examples
- warning signs
- defense mechanisms

# 21. Evidence Levels

Use a simple system:

### Level A — Strong Evidence

Multiple high-quality sources / strong scientific consensus.

### Level B — Good Evidence

Reasonably strong evidence but with limitations.

### Level C — Emerging / Mixed

Evidence exists but remains uncertain or inconsistent.

### Level D — Hypothesis

Interesting idea without sufficient evidence.

### Level E — Philosophical / Interpretive

Cannot be settled purely through empirical evidence.

This prevents philosophical claims from being presented as scientific facts.

# 30. Safety Principles

Important:

### Medical

Do not provide diagnosis.

### Mental Health

Do not present the platform as therapy.

### Sexuality

Educational and factual.

### Dark Psychology

Explain mechanisms and defenses, not exploitation instructions.

# 39. The Ultimate Principle

Open Human should not try to make people think the same.

It should help people:

> **think more consciously.**

Not:

> tell people what to believe.

But:

> **teach them how to investigate what they believe.**

Not:

> remove uncertainty.

But:

> **teach them how to live with uncertainty.**

Not:

> promise happiness.

But:

> **help them understand what happiness, suffering, meaning, and choice actually involve.**

The ultimate objective:

# **Awareness.**

A person who understands:

- their body
- their mind
- their emotions
- their biases
- their relationships
- human nature
- incentives
- power
- society
- mortality

is potentially less vulnerable to:

- misinformation
- manipulation
- unhealthy habits

- social pressure
- scams

# 40. Build Instruction for AI Coding Agent

When implementing this project:

1. Treat this document as the product vision and source of truth.
2. Do not reduce the project into a generic blog.
3. Prioritize information architecture and reading experience.
4. Build the MVP before advanced features.
5. Use Markdown/MDX for knowledge content.
6. Make knowledge pages highly readable.
7. Make navigation between concepts effortless.
8. Make evidence/source attribution visible.
9. Avoid sensationalist "dark psychology" aesthetics.
10. Avoid generic motivational aesthetics.
11. Do not over-gamify.
12. Keep the UI calm, intelligent, and editorial.
13. Build the architecture so community contributions can be added later.
14. Do not invent scientific claims merely to populate pages.
15. When writing factual content, distinguish evidence from interpretation.
16. Treat uncertainty as a feature, not a weakness.
17. Build for global users, not only Indonesia.
18. Use English as the initial source language, add Bahasa Indonesia as the first localized edition, and structure every new feature for additional locales.
19. Optimize for learning and exploration rather than maximizing session time.
20. The product should feel like a **digital library + knowledge graph + self-awareness toolkit**, not a social media platform.
